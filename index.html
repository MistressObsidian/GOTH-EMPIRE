BEFORE YOU ENTER

CLARITY IS PART OF TRUST.

Does membership include private sessions?

No. Individual sessions have separately agreed fees. Membership provides access to content, messaging and eligible booking windows.

How does private messaging work?

Allow up to 2 business days for a reply. Monthly allowances reset on the first of each month in UTC. Invitation-only arrangements are agreed personally.

Can I change my boundaries?

Yes. Edit your profile at any time. You can cancel an open booking and withdraw consent directly in your session records.
