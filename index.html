BEFORE YOU ENTER

CLARITY IS PART OF TRUST.

Does membership include private sessions?

No. Individual sessions have separately agreed fees. Membership provides access to content and eligible booking windows. Private chat is available to every registered account.

How does private messaging work?

Sign in to chat privately with the Mistress, with or without a paid membership. Chat has no membership-based message limit. Allow up to 2 business days for a reply.

Can I change my boundaries?

Yes. Tell the Mistress in private chat whenever your boundaries change. Active members can also update their profile and cancel open bookings through their dashboard.
